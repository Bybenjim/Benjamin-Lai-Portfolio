Professional Video Production Without the Complexity

Every project is different. Instead of forcing every video into a fixed production package, I build the production around what the project actually needs.

For smaller projects, that can mean a lean and efficient setup. For larger productions, the crew, equipment and specialists can scale accordingly.

The goal remains the same: create quality video content that works for the audience without making production unnecessarily complicated.

Video Production FAQ

How much does video production cost in Singapore?

It depends on the scope. A short social media video needs a lean, efficient setup, while a corporate or event production with a full crew and multi-day filming costs more. Tell me about your project and budget and I'll put together a realistic quote.

What types of videos do you produce?

Corporate videos and interviews, product and brand videos, short-form social media content, and event videography, plus editing-only work on footage you've already filmed.

Do you provide video editing only?

Yes. If you already have footage, I can handle editing, pacing, graphics, subtitles and sound without a full production.

Can you produce videos for TikTok and Instagram?

Yes, short-form vertical video built for how people actually watch on TikTok, Instagram and Reels, including cuts for paid social campaigns.

Do you handle corporate video production?

Yes. Corporate videos, leadership interviews and brand stories that communicate what a business does clearly, without unnecessary complexity.

How long does video production take?

A short social or product video can turn around in a few days. Corporate or event productions take longer once you account for planning, filming and a proper edit, usually one to three weeks depending on scope.

Looking for Video Production in Singapore?

Tell me about your project, audience and what you'd like the video to achieve. I'll help you work out the right production approach.
